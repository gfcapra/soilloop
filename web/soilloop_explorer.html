Soilloop Explorer

How contaminated soils, population health and community capacity can lock a territory into decline, and what it takes to get out.

Exploratory tool, version 0.1. The model is conceptual: its social parameters are not calibrated, so results show regimes and orders of magnitude, not the cleanup needed in a specific place. Group labels describe conditions imposed on territories, never the people who live there.

Soil

Years for the exposed layers to clear by leaching, burial and dilution. It depends on the element and on the soil: lead in organic horizons about 250 yr; cadmium about 170 yr in average EU cropland topsoils, decades in strongly leached soils.

Units

Model: dS/dt = r(1 − S) − p(1 − C)S; dH/dt = k(S − H); dC/dt = d[Hⁿ/(hⁿ + Hⁿ) − C]. r = 1/residence time, k = 1/health response time, d = 1/community recovery time. The diagnosis compares one- and two-group Gaussian mixtures (diagonal covariance) on the three scores by BIC; a ΔBIC above 10 favours two groups. A two-group pattern is consistent with the loop but does not prove it. Python package with the full analyses: soilloop 0.1, MIT license. Accompanies Capra, G.F., Breaking the loop: a dynamical model of contaminated soils, human health and community decline in marginal areas (in preparation).
